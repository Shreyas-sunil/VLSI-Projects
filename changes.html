What changed: 2024 scheme to 2026 scheme

The department replaced the 2024 scheme with the 2026 scheme. This site was rebuilt from the new PDF; this page shows what moved, so work you started under the old codes is easy to find again.

33
cards kept with the same ID
77
cards carried over under a new ID (code change or a new home course)
37
new cards written for new or reworked courses
31
2024 cards retired with dropped courses

Headline changes

  • Credits: 70 in total (23, 21, 19, 7) instead of 80 (19, 23, 18, 20). Semester I now carries the most credits.
  • Projects: the minor project and internship drop from 6 to 5 credits each; the major project drops from 18 to 5 credits but is still an 18-week individual project with mandatory publication.
  • New core courses: Integrated Microelectronics Circuits Design, AI for Engineers and Intellectual Property Rights (Sem I); VLSI Testing becomes a Sem II core; Environment and Sustainability (Sem II); UVM (Sem III).
  • Moved: SystemVerilog becomes a Sem II core; physical design and VLSI design-automation algorithms merge into one Sem II elective; Multicore Computer Architecture moves from Sem IV to Sem III.
  • Dropped: Design Thinking Lab, Skill Lab (layout), Algorithms for VLSI Design Automation as a separate core, the Professional Cluster Electives A and C, and six interdisciplinary electives.
