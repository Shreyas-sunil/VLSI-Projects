Toolchain setup

A reproducible, low-cost workflow. Commercial EDA tools are optional everywhere.

AreaRecommended toolsDocs
Simulation and verificationVerilator, Icarus Verilog, cocotb; SymbiYosys for formal; SystemVerilog classes need Questa or Riviera (free through EDA Playground) or a licensed simulator.link
UVMpyuvm on cocotb (open source) for most work; SystemVerilog UVM (IEEE 1800.2) on Questa/Riviera/VCS/Xcelium for RAL-heavy projects.link
Synthesis and FPGAYosys with nextpnr for supported FPGAs; Vivado for Xilinx devices (free edition for some parts, check current terms).link
Timing and TclOpenSTA and OpenROAD are scripted in Tcl, which matches the Tcl content of MVE311TB.link
Physical designOpenROAD-flow-scripts or OpenLane with the sky130 PDK; NanGate45/FreePDK45 are alternatives.link
Analog and layoutxschem, ngspice, Magic or KLayout with sky130; Monte-Carlo via ngspice. sky130 is not RF-characterised, so treat RF numbers as indicative.link
Embedded and firmwareSTM32F407VG Discovery (lab board), arm-none-eabi-gcc or STM32CubeIDE, OpenOCD/GDB, FreeRTOS, STM32Cube.AI or TFLite Micro, Renode for emulation. Emulators do not give true timing.link
CUDA / GPUCUDA toolkit, Nsight Compute and Nsight Systems on any supported NVIDIA GPU. Without one, run only CPU stages locally and use a hosted GPU notebook if available; CPU/OpenCL substitutes are not CUDA results and must be labelled.link
Parallel computingGCC/Clang with OpenMP, Open MPI or MPICH, STREAM, likwid or perf.link
Architecture modellingSpike, Timeloop/Accelergy, BookSim 2, SystemC, GPGPU-Sim or Accel-Sim (GPU-simulator setup is non-trivial).link
Data, ML and LLMsPython, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, PyTorch, ONNX Runtime; Ollama or llama.cpp for local LLMs (CPU is enough for the small models used here).link
CloudDocker or Podman, kind or minikube, MinIO; managed services only with free credits and budget alerts.link
Thermal and field solversElmer FEM, FreeCAD FEM, scikit-rf, openEMS.link

Hardware you may not have

  • FPGA board: optional. Use Verilator plus Yosys/Vivado reports and label results accordingly.
  • STM32F407VG Discovery: the MVE213IA lab board. Renode can emulate much of it for firmware development, but emulators do not give true timing or power.
  • NVIDIA GPU: only needed for projects marked with the CUDA note. A hosted GPU notebook is a valid way to get real CUDA numbers where available; CPU or OpenCL stand-ins are not equivalent.
  • UVM simulator: SystemVerilog UVM needs a commercial simulator (EDA Playground gives free access to some). pyuvm with cocotb is a free alternative for most MVE231TA work.
  • Commercial EDA: not needed. The open flow (Yosys, OpenROAD, ngspice, Magic) covers every project.
  • Cloud credits: optional for MCN325DR. Run everything on a local kind or minikube cluster first and set budget alerts before using a paid account.
  • Fabrication: none required; GDS from the open flow is a layout artifact, not silicon.
