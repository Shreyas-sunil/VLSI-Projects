The RVCE M.Tech VLSI Design & Embedded Systems 2024 scheme, organised by semester and course, with the full syllabus text from the PDF and links to the project cards.

Programme outcomes

  1. PO1. Independently carry out research/investigation and development work to solve practical problems related to VLSI Design and Embedded Systems.
  2. PO2. Write and present a substantial technical report/document in the field of VLSI Design and Embedded Systems.
  3. PO3. Demonstrate a degree of mastery over the area of VLSI Design and Embedded Systems, at a level higher than the bachelor's in Electronics & Communication Engineering requirements.
  4. PO4. Abstract the requirements of modern microelectronics and smart systems to offer innovative solutions with available IPs and interfaces.
  5. PO5. Design and develop VLSI and Embedded modules with good economics to meet Quality of Service.
  6. PO6. Acquire professional and intellectual integrity, research ethics and execute socio-concern projects related to modern VLSI and Embedded Systems.

   applicable to the discipline and awareness of relevant social sciences.
