SGPA and CGPA calculator

Pick your electives, enter your grades, and see SGPA per semester and CGPA overall. Credits come from the 2024 scheme tables.

Grade scale is editable and unverified. The scheme PDF does not contain a grading table. The default is the common 10-point scale (O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0). Check it against the RVCE academic regulations and change the points below if they differ. Results are for your own planning, not an official record.

How it is calculated

SGPA = Σ(credits × grade points) / Σcredits for the semester's graded courses. CGPA = the same credit-weighted average across all graded courses in all semesters (not an average of SGPAs). Courses left as "Not graded" are ignored. Re-exam, grade-improvement and pass/fail rules are not modelled.
