Checked against the 2026 scheme

  • Group A elective: only MVE315AX "Digital System Design using FPGA" is timetabled, which matches MVE315AA Digital Systems Design and FPGA Implementation. The other Group A options (MVE315AB, AC, AD) are not offered to this class.
  • Codes: the timetable prints MVE2121A and MVE2131A where the scheme has MVE212IA and MVE213IA (most likely "IA" typed as "1A"), and MVE216TA/B or MVE216TB where the scheme has MCS216TB for AI for Engineers (it is a CS-board course common to several programmes). This site keeps the scheme codes.
  • Titles: "Static Timing Analysis & Scripting" and "Digital System Design using FPGA" are short forms of the scheme titles; "Intellectual Property Right" is "Intellectual Property Rights" in the scheme.
  • Labs: AICD is taught by MGR, which matches Dr. Govinda Raju M. of MVE213IA, so it is most likely the MVE213IA (embedded) lab; LPD is the MVE212IA low-power design lab. Each batch gets one 2-hour session per week for each lab, which matches the P = 1 credit (2 hours) of both courses. The AICD expansion is not given on the timetable; confirm it.
  • Skill Lab: Friday 2:30-4:30 has a Skill Lab (SSN, SSH), but the 2026 scheme has no Skill Lab and allots it no credits. Ask the department whether it is graded or counted anywhere.
  • Hours vs credits: MVE311TB and MVE314TA (3:1:0) get 4 slots each, MCS216TB (2:0:0) gets 2 and HSS217TA (1:0:0) gets 1, all as expected. MVE212IA and MVE213IA (3:0:1) get 4 theory slots plus a 2-hour lab. MVE315AA (3:0:0) gets 4 slots, one more than its 3 lecture credits. Monday 3:30-4:30 and Saturday after 11:30 are free.

All 8 Semester I subjects in the scheme appear on the timetable, plus a Skill Lab that the scheme does not list. For what to build this semester, see the Semester I plan.
